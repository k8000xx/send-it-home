Sign on Stop 1 of 5

Before a seafarer joins a ship they sign a contract. It says what job they will do, how many months they will sail, and how much they earn each month. Pick your job aboard the MV Sea Star — a 9 month voyage.

Payday at sea Stop 2 of 5

There is no cash machine in the middle of the ocean! On payday the shipping company loads your wage straight onto your crew card, and your app buzzes to tell you. Tap the coins to load your card — but do not tap the fake messages 📩 that try to trick you into giving your money away.

Share out your pay Stop 3 of 5

Now the big choice. Every month a seafarer decides how much of the wage goes home to the family. That share is called an allotment, and it goes home every single month without anyone having to ask. Fill all three jars — every dollar must go somewhere.

🏠 Your family's monthly bills:
  • Rent for the house — $200
  • Food for the family — $250
  • School for your little sister — $150
So the Family jar needs at least $600. Savings needs at least a tenth of your wage, and you need at least $50 in your pocket for soap, snacks and phone data at sea.

The Transfer Marketplace Stop 4 of 5

Your family lives thousands of miles away. Money sent home like this is called a remittance. Your app shows you several ways to send your $0 side by side, so you can compare the fee and how long each one takes. Which one leaves the most money for your family?

Family day Stop 5 of 5

The money has arrived on your family's companion card — a second card, linked to yours, that they keep at home. Help them shop: buy every need first, then decide about the fun things. Whatever is left stays on the card as savings.
